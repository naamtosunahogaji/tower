Ironpath Bastion is a focused medieval tower defense campaign with isometric 2D presentation, strategic tower combinations, branching routes, and controlled physics-driven cannon impacts. The first build concentrates on one polished three-level forest chapter designed for reliable desktop play.
Genre
2D isometric tower defense
Core Loop
Spend gold to place and upgrade complementary towers, start a wave, watch defenses engage advancing enemies, then use earned rewards to adapt before the next assault. Complete levels to earn stars and unlock harder maps and mechanics.
Rules
Begin each level with 300 gold and 20 lives; each escaping enemy removes one life.
Tower placement snaps to valid build zones and may not overlap roads, obstacles, or other towers.
The initial roster contains Archer, Cannon, and Support towers, each with three upgrade levels.
Archer towers specialize in single targets, Cannons deal area damage and knockback, and Support towers improve nearby defenses.
Destroyed enemies award gold; selling a tower refunds 80% of its total investment.
Each level contains 10 waves, including an elite wave and a final boss wave.
Difficulty increases through stronger compositions rather than excessive enemy speed.
Controls
Mouse: select towers, place, upgrade, sell, and inspect targets.
Right mouse button or Escape: cancel placement or close selection.
WASD or arrow keys: pan the battlefield camera.
Mouse wheel: zoom in or out.
Space: begin the next wave.
P or Escape: pause while between waves; R restarts after victory or defeat.
More
Win
Survive all waves with at least one life. Remaining lives and whether any tower was sold determine a one-to-three-star rating and unlock the next level.
Lose
The level ends in defeat when lives reach zero; retrying fully resets towers, waves, lives, and level gold.
Key Objects
Player keep and enemy exit
Buildable plots and blocked terrain
Archer, Cannon, and Support towers
Tower projectiles and support auras
Goblin, Orc, Troll, Shaman, and chapter boss enemies
Branching enemy roads
Gold rewards, wave spawners, and environmental obstacles
Visual Direction
Clean Canvas-rendered 2D isometric artwork that evokes low-poly medieval fantasy through faceted shapes, restrained toon shading, and strong silhouettes. Use a readable forest palette, clearly separated roads and build zones, modest impact effects, and subtle height cues without obscuring gameplay.
UI Direction
A compact top bar for lives, gold, and wave progress; a small tower palette with costs; contextual upgrade and sell actions beside the selected tower; a next-wave button; concise tooltips and victory or defeat results.
Avoid
Mixing medieval and science-fiction themes in the initial chapter.
Allowing towers or enemies to block or alter the required route.
Uncontrolled physics that pushes enemies off-path or causes unstable collisions.
Excessive particles, damage numbers, camera movement, or decorative panels.
Large enemy-speed increases that make targeting unreadable.
Too many towers, levels, currencies, or progression systems in the first playable build.
